APPOINTMENT SCHEDULING POLICY
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Department: Patient Access & Scheduling
Regulatory Basis: Clinical Establishments Act, 2010 | Consumer Protection Act, 2019

OVERVIEW
This policy governs the scheduling, rescheduling, and cancellation of outpatient (OPD),
specialist, and telemedicine appointments at this healthcare facility, in compliance with the
Clinical Establishments (Registration and Regulation) Act, 2010. The facility is registered
with the respective State Clinical Establishments Authority and is NABH accredited.

HOW TO BOOK AN APPOINTMENT
Patients may book appointments through the following channels:
1. Patient Portal (Recommended): Log in at myhealth.healthsystem.in, select "Book Appointment,"
   choose your department, doctor, and preferred slot. Confirmation is sent via SMS and email.
2. Helpline: Call 1800-123-4567 (Toll Free), available Monday–Saturday, 8am–8pm.
3. ABDM / ABHA Integration: Patients with an Ayushman Bharat Health Account (ABHA ID) may
   book appointments through the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) Health app or
   partner platforms (e.g., UMANG app).
4. In-Person: Visit the OPD Registration Desk, Ground Floor, Main Building.
5. Telemedicine: Telemedicine slots can be booked via the patient portal or helpline. A
   secure video link is shared 30 minutes before the appointment.

APPOINTMENT TYPES
- New Patient OPD: First consultation at this facility. Bring all previous medical records,
  prescriptions, and investigation reports. Duration: 30–45 minutes.
- Follow-up OPD: Review visits for ongoing treatment. Duration: 15–20 minutes.
- Specialist Referral: Requires a referral letter from a General Physician or General
  Practitioner. Some insurance plans and PMJAY require a GP referral for specialist visits.
- Day Care Procedure Slot: For procedures that do not require overnight admission (e.g.,
  endoscopy, cataract surgery, chemotherapy session). Requires prior booking and pre-procedure
  clearance.
- Emergency OPD: Available 24x7 for urgent but non-life-threatening conditions. Walk-in basis.
  For life-threatening emergencies, call 112.

ESTIMATED WAITING TIMES (NEW PATIENT APPOINTMENTS)
- General Medicine: 1–3 working days
- Cardiology: 5–7 working days
- Orthopaedics: 4–7 working days
- Dermatology: 7–14 working days
- Psychiatry/Mental Health: 3–5 working days (urgent slots available)
- Oncology: 3–5 working days (expedited on medical urgency)
- All other specialties: 5–10 working days

For urgent medical conditions that cannot wait, please use the Emergency OPD or call the
facility helpline for priority booking.

CANCELLATION AND RESCHEDULING POLICY
- Appointments must be cancelled at least 24 hours in advance to avoid a cancellation fee
  of ₹200 (waived for documented medical emergencies).
- Cancellations can be made via the patient portal, helpline, or in person.
- Repeated no-shows (3 or more in 12 months) may result in a requirement for advance payment
  at the time of booking.
- Rescheduling is free of charge and can be done any time before the appointment.

AYUSHMAN BHARAT (PMJAY) AND GOVERNMENT SCHEME PATIENTS
- Patients covered under Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PMJAY/Ayushman Bharat) must
  present their e-card or Ayushman card at registration.
- PMJAY benefits are available for empanelled procedures only. Please confirm with the
  insurance desk before scheduling a procedure.
- Beneficiaries of state government schemes (e.g., Mahatma Jyotiba Phule Jan Arogya Yojana
  in Maharashtra) should present the relevant scheme card at registration.

PATIENT RIGHTS UNDER THE CONSUMER PROTECTION ACT, 2019
Patients have the right to receive timely, transparent appointment services. Complaints
regarding scheduling deficiencies may be filed with the Patient Relations Officer or escalated
to the District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission.
