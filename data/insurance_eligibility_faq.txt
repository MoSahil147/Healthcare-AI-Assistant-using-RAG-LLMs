INSURANCE & HEALTH SCHEME ELIGIBILITY FAQ
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Department: Patient Financial Services & Billing
Regulatory Basis: IRDAI Regulations | PMJAY Guidelines | ABDM Framework

OVERVIEW
This document answers frequently asked questions about health insurance, government health
schemes, cashless claims, and billing at this facility. The facility is empanelled under
Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PMJAY/Ayushman Bharat) and is registered with major
Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI)-regulated insurers.

Q: WHAT HEALTH INSURANCE PLANS AND GOVERNMENT SCHEMES DOES THIS FACILITY ACCEPT?
A: This facility accepts:
- Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PMJAY / Ayushman Bharat): Up to ₹5 lakh per family per year.
- State government health schemes: Mahatma Jyotiba Phule Jan Arogya Yojana (Maharashtra),
  Chief Minister's Comprehensive Health Insurance Scheme, and other state-specific schemes.
- Central Government Health Scheme (CGHS): For central government employees and pensioners.
- ECHS (Ex-Servicemen Contributory Health Scheme): For armed forces veterans.
- All major private insurers: Star Health, New India Assurance, HDFC ERGO, Care Health,
  Niva Bupa (formerly Max Bupa), ICICI Lombard, and others.
- Corporate group health policies via TPA (Third-Party Administrators) such as Medi Assist,
  Paramount, Health India, and Vipul Medcorp.
Please contact the Insurance Desk at 1800-123-5678 to confirm your specific plan.

Q: HOW DO I VERIFY MY ELIGIBILITY BEFORE ADMISSION OR CONSULTATION?
A: Eligibility can be verified through:
- Patient Portal: Log in at myhealth.healthsystem.in → "Insurance & Billing" → "Check Eligibility."
- Insurance Desk: Visit the Insurance Desk (Ground Floor, near Registration) with your policy
  document, health card, and a valid photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, or Passport).
- Helpline: Call 1800-123-5678 (Mon–Sat 8am–6pm).
- ABHA ID: Patients linked to the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission can check coverage through
  the ABDM Health app using their ABHA (Health ID) number.
We recommend verifying eligibility at least 48 hours before a planned procedure.

Q: HOW DOES CASHLESS HOSPITALISATION WORK?
A: For cashless treatment:
1. Inform the Insurance Desk at the time of admission (or 48 hours before for planned procedures).
2. The desk submits a pre-authorisation request to your insurer or TPA.
3. The insurer/TPA approves or queries the request within 2–4 hours (pre-planned) or
   1–2 hours (emergency under IRDAI's cashless discharge guidelines).
4. Upon approval, you are not required to pay the approved amount upfront. You pay only
   deductibles, co-payments, or non-payable items as specified in your policy.
As per IRDAI's Cashless Authorisation Master Circular (2024), insurers must process
pre-authorisation for planned hospitalisations within 1 hour of receiving complete documents.

Q: WHAT IS A CO-PAYMENT AND DOES MY POLICY HAVE ONE?
A: A co-payment (co-pay) is the fixed percentage of the claim amount that you pay from your
own pocket, as defined in your policy:
- Senior citizen health plans often have a mandatory co-pay of 10–20%.
- Zone-upgrade co-pay applies if you choose a room category higher than your policy entitlement.
- Some corporate policies have a co-pay for specific conditions or treatments.
Check your policy's Schedule of Benefits or call your insurer to confirm your co-pay obligations.
This facility will inform you of any applicable co-pay at the time of admission.

Q: WHAT IS PRE-AUTHORISATION AND WHEN IS IT REQUIRED?
A: Pre-authorisation (also called prior approval or PA) is mandatory for:
- All planned surgical procedures and day-care treatments
- Chemotherapy, dialysis, and radiation therapy sessions
- High-value investigations (MRI, CT, PET scan) when covered under hospitalisation
- ICU admissions and organ transplants
The facility's Insurance Desk initiates the PA process on your behalf. Processing typically
takes 2–6 hours. IRDAI mandates that insurers cannot refuse cashless authorisation without
adequate grounds.

Q: WHAT ITEMS ARE NOT COVERED UNDER STANDARD HEALTH INSURANCE?
A: Standard exclusions under most IRDAI-regulated policies include:
- OPD (outpatient) consultations, unless the policy has an OPD rider
- Cosmetic or aesthetic procedures not medically necessary
- Dental treatment (unless due to accidental injury)
- Spectacles, hearing aids, and contact lenses
- Maternity expenses (unless maternity rider is included, with waiting period)
- Pre-existing diseases during the initial waiting period (typically 2–4 years)
- Self-inflicted injuries
Refer to your specific policy wording for a complete exclusions list.

Q: HOW DOES PMJAY (AYUSHMAN BHARAT) WORK AT THIS FACILITY?
A: PMJAY provides health coverage of up to ₹5 lakh per family per year for secondary and
tertiary hospitalisation. To avail benefits:
- Present your Ayushman Card (PM-JAY e-card) or verify eligibility via ABHA ID at the desk.
- Benefits are cashless and paperless — the facility bills the government directly.
- PMJAY covers over 1,929 procedures (HBP packages) including surgeries, dialysis, and
  cancer treatment. OPD visits are not covered under standard PMJAY.
- Beneficiaries can check eligibility at pmjay.gov.in or on the UMANG app.

Q: WHAT IF I CANNOT AFFORD MY HOSPITAL BILL?
A: No patient is turned away due to inability to pay for emergency care, as mandated by the
Clinical Establishments Act, 2010 and Supreme Court directives on emergency treatment.
Financial assistance options available:
- Facility-operated patient welfare fund for BPL (Below Poverty Line) patients
- Instalment-based payment plans (0% interest for up to 6 months)
- Assistance with PMJAY enrolment for eligible patients
Contact the Financial Counsellor at financial.counsellor@healthsystem.in or visit Room 102.

Q: HOW DO I RAISE A BILLING DISPUTE?
A: Billing disputes must be raised within 30 days of receiving the final bill by contacting
Patient Financial Services at billing@healthsystem.in or calling 1800-123-5678. If unresolved,
patients may escalate to the IRDAI Grievance Cell (igms.irda.gov.in) or the Consumer Disputes
Redressal Commission under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.
