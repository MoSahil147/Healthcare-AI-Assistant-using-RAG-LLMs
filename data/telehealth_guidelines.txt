TELEMEDICINE CONSULTATION GUIDELINES
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Department: Patient Services & Digital Health
Regulatory Basis: Telemedicine Practice Guidelines, 2020 (MoHFW & NMC)

OVERVIEW
These guidelines govern the delivery of telemedicine services at this healthcare facility in
accordance with the Telemedicine Practice Guidelines issued jointly by the Ministry of Health
and Family Welfare (MoHFW) and the National Medical Commission (NMC) on March 25, 2020.
Telemedicine enables registered medical practitioners (RMPs) to provide medical advice,
diagnosis support, and prescriptions through audio, video, or text-based digital platforms.

ELIGIBILITY FOR TELEMEDICINE CONSULTATIONS
Telemedicine consultations are available to:
- Established patients with at least one prior in-person visit on record with this facility.
- Patients in remote or rural areas who cannot access in-person care conveniently.
- Patients requiring follow-up consultations for chronic conditions such as diabetes,
  hypertension, thyroid disorders, or asthma.
- Patients seeking mental health consultations (subject to Mental Healthcare Act, 2017).

New patients may access telemedicine only for first-aid advice, follow-up of a referral, or
in cases where in-person visit is not immediately possible. A detailed in-person visit must
follow at the earliest opportunity.

REGISTERED MEDICAL PRACTITIONER REQUIREMENTS
As per NMC Telemedicine Guidelines 2020, only a registered medical practitioner (RMP) holding
a valid registration with the National Medical Commission or State Medical Council is authorised
to conduct telemedicine consultations and issue prescriptions. The RMP must:
- Verify patient identity at the start of every telemedicine session.
- Maintain a proper record of every telemedicine interaction.
- Provide their name, registration number, and contact details to the patient.

PRESCRIPTION RULES UNDER TELEMEDICINE
Prescriptions issued via telemedicine must comply with the following:
- The RMP may prescribe medications from List O (over-the-counter) and List A (specified
  medicines) through telemedicine, subject to clinical appropriateness.
- List B medicines (including Schedule H and H1 drugs under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940)
  may only be prescribed via telemedicine for follow-up patients where the condition is already
  diagnosed and stable.
- Narcotic and psychotropic substances listed under the NDPS Act, 1985 (e.g., morphine,
  tramadol, benzodiazepines) CANNOT be prescribed via telemedicine under any circumstances.
- Prescriptions must be issued on a signed letterhead or via the facility's digital prescription
  system and transmitted to the patient securely.

PERMITTED TELEMEDICINE SERVICES
The following may be provided via telemedicine:
- Follow-up consultations for chronic disease management
- Mental health therapy and psychiatry (non-NDPS medication management)
- Dermatology consultations via photo/video review
- Nutritional and dietary counselling
- Post-surgical follow-up where physical wound inspection is not required
- Review and renewal of existing prescriptions (non-NDPS)

TECHNOLOGY AND PRIVACY REQUIREMENTS
- All telemedicine sessions must be conducted on secure, encrypted platforms.
- Patient data collected during telemedicine must be stored and processed in compliance with
  the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA) and the IT Act, 2000.
- WhatsApp and similar consumer messaging apps may be used only for text/image consultations;
  they are not permitted for video consultations involving clinical assessment.
- Audio or video recording of consultations without explicit patient consent is prohibited.

LIMITATIONS AND EXCLUSIONS
Telemedicine is NOT appropriate for:
- Medical emergencies — patients must call 112 or visit the nearest emergency facility.
- Conditions requiring physical examination, diagnostic investigation, or surgical assessment.
- First-time prescription of Schedule X drugs or NDPS-listed substances.
- Termination of pregnancy guidance (subject to MTP Act, 2021).

PATIENT GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL
Complaints regarding telemedicine services may be filed with:
- The facility's Patient Relations Officer at patientcare@healthsystem.in
- The National Medical Commission at www.nmc.org.in
- The Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.
